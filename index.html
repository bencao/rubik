Solving the 3×3 cube, layer by layer

Seven steps from a scrambled cube to a solved one, each with a picture of what to look for and the formula to use.

Drag any cube to rotate it.Tap the rotate button on a cube, then drag to turn it.

    Reading the formulas

    Each letter names a face. Turn it a quarter turn clockwise as if you were looking straight at that face. Keep white on the bottom and yellow on top; “front” faces you.

    clockwise quarter turn
    prime: counter-clockwise
    half turn (either way)
    Step 01

    Bottom cross

    底棱归位 · white cross

    Target

    White cross on the bottom. Each white edge's side color matches the center above it.

    Part 1: make a daisy

    Daisy

    Keep yellow on top. Bring all four white edges up around the yellow center, white facing up. Their other colors don't matter yet.

    Before every move: turn the top (U) until the top spot where the piece will land is empty, so you don't knock off a petal you already placed.

    Part 2: turn the daisy into the cross

    Turn the top (U) until a petal's side color matches the center below it. Hold that side as the front, then:

    The white edge drops to the bottom. Do the same for all four petals, turning the whole cube (yellow stays on top) to bring each one to the front.

    Bottom corners

    Target
